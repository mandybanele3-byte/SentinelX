      CYBERSECURITY LEARNING

      How to spot job scams

      01

      Never pay to get hired

      Be cautious when a recruiter asks for visa fees, training fees, equipment payments, gift cards, or cryptocurrency.

      02

      Check the company identity

      Compare the recruiter email domain with the official company website and contact the company directly.

      03

      Protect personal documents

      Do not send passport copies, bank details, passwords, or one-time codes before independently verifying the employer.

      04

      Watch for unrealistic promises

      Very high salaries, instant hiring, no interview, and vague job duties are common warning signs.

      05

      Be careful with messaging apps

      Legitimate employers may use WhatsApp or Telegram, but app-only recruitment with no verifiable company contact is risky.

      06

      Pause before clicking

      Inspect links carefully and avoid downloading unexpected interview files or software.
